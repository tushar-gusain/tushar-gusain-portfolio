CASE STUDY 01 · PLATFORM ARCHITECTURE

Domain Separation &
Multi-Tenant Architecture

Implemented a multi-tenant ServiceNow architecture supporting 10+ portfolio companies—improving data segregation, simplifying administration and enabling repeatable onboarding across different service models.

DOMAIN SEPARATIONARCHITECTURE POCSTAKEHOLDER ALIGNMENT10+ COMPANIES

01 / THE CHALLENGE

One shared platform. Multiple operating contexts.

Multiple portfolio companies were operating on a shared ServiceNow instance without adequate data segregation. The platform needed a stronger tenancy model while remaining manageable for the central team and practical to onboard for new companies.

02 / ARCHITECTURE APPROACH

Establishing a repeatable tenant model

I identified Domain Separation as the approach to evaluate, developed a proof of concept and presented the benefits to stakeholders, including the CTO. Following stakeholder buy-in, I led technical planning and delivery with development, testing and business-analysis resources.
